LuxeLane Customer Retention Analysis
Project Overview

LuxeLane is a fictional e-commerce fashion business experiencing challenges with customer retention and repeat purchases.

This project analyzes customer behaviour, complaints, delivery experience, returns, satisfaction and purchasing activity to understand why customers churn and which customers may require attention.

Tools: Excel, Power Query, PivotTables, PivotCharts

Dataset: 2,508 records representing 2,500 unique customers.

Business Problem

The business needed to understand:

How many customers are being retained or lost?
Which customer segments have higher churn?
What problems are associated with customer churn?
Which customers should be prioritized for retention?

Key Findings

The analysis identified several customer issues associated with churn, including:

Unresolved customer issues
Product-related problems
Discount issues
Customer service problems
Delivery problems
Pricing and competitor-related factors

The results show that customer churn is not linked to one single issue. Different parts of the customer experience need to be considered when developing retention strategies.

Recommendations

Based on the analysis, LuxeLane should:

Improve complaint resolution and reduce unresolved issues.
Monitor at-risk customers before they churn.
Investigate recurring delivery problems.
Review product quality and product descriptions.
Pay closer attention to high-value customers showing reduced activity.
Track customer satisfaction and repeat-purchase behaviour regularly.
